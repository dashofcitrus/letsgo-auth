Privacy policy

letsgo is a personal app used by one person: its owner. It connects to the owner's own Oura and Withings accounts to read sleep, activity, workout and weight data.

  • Data is stored only on the owner's private server, which is not reachable from the public internet.
  • Data is never sold, shared or used for advertising.
  • Meal photos and descriptions may be sent to an AI provider (OpenAI or Anthropic) solely to estimate nutrition. Daily summaries may be sent to an AI provider to generate coaching messages.
  • Access can be revoked at any time from the Oura or Withings account settings, or from within the app.
